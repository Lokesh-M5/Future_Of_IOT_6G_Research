# Future of IoT: 6G Research

## Overview

This repository explores how **6G (IMT-2030)** could transform the Internet of Things (IoT) by enabling massive device connectivity, ultra-low-latency communication, AI-native networking, edge intelligence, integrated sensing, improved positioning, and more sustainable connected systems.

The project focuses on the **research and future potential of 6G-enabled IoT**, rather than claiming to implement a real 6G network.

## Research Topic

**AI-Assisted Edge Intelligence for Massive IoT in 6G Networks**

### Core Research Question

> How can 6G technologies make IoT systems more intelligent, scalable, reliable, and energy-efficient?

## Objectives

* Study the evolution of IoT from 4G/5G toward 6G.
* Analyze the IMT-2030 framework and expected 6G capabilities.
* Investigate massive IoT connectivity.
* Study AI-native and intelligent network architectures.
* Explore Edge AI and distributed computing for IoT.
* Investigate Integrated Sensing and Communication (ISAC).
* Examine digital twins and future IoT applications.
* Analyze security, privacy, reliability, and sustainability challenges.
* Identify research gaps and potential future research directions.
* Evaluate latency, scalability, network traffic, and energy-related trade-offs through simulation where applicable.

## Key Research Areas

### 🤖 AI-Native Networking

Exploring how artificial intelligence can become part of network management, optimization, resource allocation, prediction, and automation.

### ⚡ Edge Intelligence

Studying how processing IoT data closer to devices can reduce latency, network traffic, and dependence on centralized cloud infrastructure.

### 🌐 Massive IoT

Investigating how future networks could support extremely high densities of connected sensors, machines, and intelligent devices.

### 📡 Integrated Sensing and Communication

Exploring how future wireless networks could simultaneously provide communication and sensing capabilities for applications such as smart cities, robotics, transportation, and industrial IoT.

### 🌍 Ubiquitous Connectivity

Studying the combination of terrestrial and non-terrestrial networks to provide IoT connectivity in remote and difficult-to-reach environments.

### 🔐 Security & Privacy

Examining challenges associated with massive numbers of devices, distributed AI, edge computing, data privacy, authentication, and network resilience.

### 🌱 Sustainable IoT

Investigating how future IoT networks can reduce communication and computation energy consumption while supporting large-scale deployments.

## Expected Outcome

The expected outcome is a research-based analysis of how 6G technologies can address limitations of current IoT networks, supported by literature, proposed architecture, simulations or experiments where applicable, and quantitative performance analysis.

## Important Disclaimer

This project does **not** represent a commercial 6G network implementation.

6G is currently under research and standardization through the **ITU IMT-2030 framework** and related industry standardization activities. Performance values discussed in this repository should therefore be understood as targets, research directions, simulations, or literature findings rather than guaranteed commercial performance.
